future AppImage application pattern When a needed desktop app has no supported Omarchy, official-repo, AUR, or Flatpak route Treat an AppImage as an explicit application decision, not a hand-managed .desktop file. Prefer native-AUR gearlever for integration only: it owns the menu entry and icon, while normal AUR updates maintain Gear Lever itself. Keep the image unextracted unless the candidate is proven compatible with extraction, then evaluate its updater before enabling any second updater:
  • Check for vendor self-updating, standard AppImage .upd_info, and the expected update trigger. Preserve a normal APPIMAGE launch environment when the vendor updater needs it.
  • Do not assume Gear Lever can provide background updates. The installed Omarchy portal backends do not expose the XDG Background portal, and its background setting checks/notifies rather than silently installing updates.
  • Avoid a generic automatic-update/extraction manager when it would duplicate a working vendor updater. AppImageLauncher v3 remains beta and appimaged is deprecated; AppManager needs a separate candidate-specific evaluation.
  • Record vendor provenance, update behavior, desktop launch flags, and security review before adoption. If accepted, add gearlever to packages.aur.txt and document the resulting managed behavior through the normal decision/finding flow.
Initial candidate: Traycer Desktop. Its current official AppImage has no standard .upd_info, but its Electron updater checks on launch/resume and requires a user-confirmed download and restart. Its vendor desktop entry uses --no-sandbox; review that risk and the vendor updater-credential exposure before installation (no credential is recorded in this repo). Sources: Traycer install, Gear Lever, electron-builder updates, and AppImage updates.
